# Goal 9A isolated water and boiler regression

Run `python -B -X utf8 tools/migration/verify_goal9a.py` with the existing Python and JDK 21. The script finds the JDK through `JAVA_HOME`, then `C:\Program Files\Java\jdk-21`, then the `PATH`. Each run writes its commands, compiler output and assertions to a distinct `build/migration/goal9a/regression/<id>/result.log`, prints the checks per section and the total, and refuses to run if any stand-in would shadow a production source. javac receives its file list through `sources.txt` in the run folder, so long temporary source paths stay under the Windows command-line limit.

A failed assertion is printed as `AssertionError in <section> ...`; the remaining sections still run, and the run fails at the end if any section failed. Passed checks are counted, failed ones are not.

`python -B -X utf8 tools/migration/goal9a_mutation_check.py` reverts eighteen Goal 9A decisions one at a time in a temporary copy of `src/main/java` under `build/migration/goal9a` and reruns the regression (log: `build/migration/goal9a/mutation_check.log`). The unmutated baseline must pass first. A mutation counts as detected only when the regression reports an `AssertionError`; a mutation that does not compile is reported as an error. `--source DIR` mutates another source root (for example a copy with a proposed fix) and `--log FILE` writes elsewhere.

`python -B -X utf8 tools/migration/verify_goal9a_resources.py --source-only` checks the anvil entry (including that `AnvilRecipes.java` without the boiler block and the explicit entries later goals append after it is identical to the Goal 8 commit `1d37f11b`), the two container registrations, both render angle tables against the original, the boiler block's proxy, identifier and refund drop-chance code, the English description, the three Chinese values, the localization records and the reused asset hashes. Without `--source-only` (after `runData`) it also compares the generated output with `build/migration/goal9a/stamp-before-g9a.json`. `package_goal9a.py` checks and keeps the built JAR after that, relative to the Goal 8 release.

Section 8 also compiles `AnvilRecipes.java` as it was at the Goal 8 commit `1d37f11b` (read with `git show`), moved to the package `goal9a.head`, and compares every earlier construction entry with the live list. Goal 10B (`migration/GOAL10B.md` D1, D6) corrects seven of those entries in place: the soldering station takes 4 tungsten bolts instead of 1, and the titanium and steel flat stamps make their own plate/wire/circuit stamps. `goal10b()` in the fixture applies exactly that change to the Goal 8 descriptor before the comparison and requires exactly seven corrected entries; every other entry is still compared in content and index. `verify_goal9a_resources.py` puts the same seven source lines back (each must occur once) before its byte comparison with `1d37f11b`.

## Stand-ins

`goal9a_regression/stubs.py` starts from the Goal 8 stand-ins and then:

- replaces the item component storage with the vanilla model. The semantics come from the Minecraft 1.21.1 sources in `build/moddev/artifacts/neoforge-21.1.228-sources.jar`, not from this regression: `Items.java:1214-1215` and `1366-1369` (bucket stacks to 16; water bucket to 1 with the bucket as remainder; the potion stacks to 1 and has the default component `POTION_CONTENTS = PotionContents.EMPTY`, so a water bottle's water is a patch; the glass bottle has no such default), `ItemStack.transmuteCopy`/`transmuteCopyIgnoreEmpty` (the copy is the new item's prototype plus the source's patch), `PatchedDataComponentMap.set` (a value equal to the prototype drops the patch entry), `PatchedDataComponentMap.remove` (writes `Optional.empty()` only when the prototype has the type, otherwise drops the patch entry), `applyPatch`/`fromPatch` (sanitised the same way), `DataComponentPatch.entrySet`, `ItemStack.isSameItemSameComponents` (compares prototype and patch) and `PotionContents` (a record compared by value; `createItemStack` sets the component). Section 1 starts with five checks that the stand-in follows these rules; they are model checks, not production checks. `customTag` and damage stay separate fields as in the Goal 8 stand-in and are not part of the component comparison;
- makes `NtmDataComponents.META` a component type, adds `Potions.WATER/AWKWARD/HEALING` as value holders and the four vanilla container items;
- registers the boiler block-entity types and sounds, adds `DummyableBlock.safeRem` and `BlockPos.above()`;
- records world effects instead of simulating them: `Level.removeBlock` stores the position with the `safeRem` value at that moment and drops the block and its entity; `ExplosionVNT` stores what the production builder receives (position, size, exploder, entity processor, SFX, block allocator/processor) and the `safeRem` value when `explode()` runs. `EntityProcessorStandard.withRangeMod` stores the range mod. The Goal 8 static helpers stay for the Stirling engine and stray cog;
- compiles the production `FT_Heatable` on a minimal `FluidTrait` and gson surface, and appends the heatable fluids and their products to the `Fluids` stand-in with the `FT_Heatable` traits parsed from production `Fluids.java` (water: BOILER 1.0, first step 200 TU + 1 mB -> 100 mB steam; oil: 10 TU + 1 mB -> 1 mB hot oil; coolant has no BOILER efficiency). The Goal 8 fluid identities keep their IDs.

The Goal 5C stand-ins (base of the Goal 6, 7B and 8 suites) gained only inert declarations so those suites compile the changed `FluidContainerRegistry`: `DataComponentType`, an empty `DataComponentPatch.entrySet()` (every registered container keeps the META-only matching those suites assert), `PotionContents.createItemStack` returning the plain item, `Potions.WATER` and the four vanilla items as identities. The Goal 3A anvil stand-ins gained `NtmTags.Items.PLATES_COPPER`, used by the new anvil entry.

## Sections

1. Water containers through the real registry and `FluidTank.loadTank/unloadTank`: water bucket <-> bucket 1000 mB, water bottle <-> glass bottle 250 mB in that order before the NTM containers; the emptied bottle has no `POTION_CONTENTS` and stacks with plain glass bottles; a named bottle keeps its name; awkward, healing and plain potions are not water; buckets and bottles do nothing for other fluids; bucket and glass bottle are registered empty containers; every NTM lead tank, universal tank and fluid barrel keeps META-only matching, 1000/1000/16000 mB and plain empty containers.
2. Heat pull with a recording heat source: `ceil(diff * 0.1)`, caps 3,200,000 and 12,800,000, decay `max(heat/1000, 1)` without and with a colder source, `diff == 0` neither pulls nor decays, no decay and no positive pull at the cap, a pre-Goal 9A heat above the cap decays with a colder source and falls to the cap with a hotter one without passing a negative amount to `useUpHeat`.
3. Conversion: tank sizes from the input type (water 1,600,000 mB steam, oil 16,000 mB hot oil, industrial 6,400,000), `ops = min(water, free/100, heat/200)`, 200 TU per mB, `isOn`, oil at 10 TU per mB.
4. Explosion: 99 mB free with no heat and no water explodes, exactly 100 mB free does not, a full steam tank does even with water and heat; exactly the 19 blocks, each removed under `safeRem`, which is reset afterwards; one `ExplosionVNT` at (x+0.5, y+2, z+0.5), strength 5, `EntityProcessorStandard` with range mod 3, only `ExplosionEffectStandard`, no block allocator or processor, run while `safeRem` is set; the heat is kept; afterwards no pull, conversion or steam provision while sync continues; idempotent; `hasExploded` saved, loaded, kept in the item NBT and synced; the refund is null intact and a fresh mutable [4 steel ingots, 8 copper plates] when exploded; the industrial boiler never explodes.
5. `trySetInputType`: water accepted and marked for saving; creosote (no `FT_Heatable`), coolant (no BOILER efficiency) and NONE rejected with the water type and fill untouched; oil and air accepted.
6. Ports for all four facings: clockwise axis, ports at core +-rot*2 facing outwards and (x, y+4, z) up; a water barrel feeds one side through a duct, steam reaches barrels behind the other side and on top, with stored steam equal to 100 x water used; ducts at the earlier +-facing*2 positions connect to nothing.
7. Barrel slot rules: slot 2 takes full containers of the tank type within capacity (a 12,000 mB barrel rejects a 16,000 mB fluid barrel item), slot 4 empty fillable containers; slots 0/1 identifiers only; face slots and extraction rules; one tick loading a water bucket from slot 2 and filling a bucket in slot 4.
8. Anvil: exactly one boiler entry, directly after the Goal 8 list (Goal 9B appends the steam engine after it); every earlier entry equal in content and index to the Goal 8 list; the Stirling block directly before the boiler; the boiler entry's inputs (4 steel ingots by item, 16 copper plates by tag, 8 insulator plates), output, tier 2 and construction overlay.
9. Chain: coal firebox (ticked first) -> boiler, water barrel -> duct -> boiler -> duct -> steam store for 1,200 ticks; every tick steam made = 100 x water used and heat change = pull - 200 x ops; the last 200 ticks steady at 1 mB/t from 200 TU/t; nothing lost; saved after tick 600 and reloaded, the chain matches the original run to tick 1,200; a sealed boiler with 16,000 mB of water explodes at the tick an independent model of the firebox and boiler formulas predicts (16,010), with 1,600,000 mB of steam.
