# Goal 9B isolated steam engine and condenser regression

Run `python -B -X utf8 tools/migration/verify_goal9b.py` with the existing Python and JDK 21. The script finds the JDK through `JAVA_HOME`, then `C:\Program Files\Java\jdk-21`, then the `PATH`. Each run writes its commands, compiler output and assertions to a distinct `build/migration/goal9b/regression/<id>/result.log`, prints the checks per section and the total, and refuses to run if any stand-in would shadow a production source. javac receives its file list through `sources.txt` in the run folder, so long temporary source paths stay under the Windows command-line limit.

A failed assertion is printed as `AssertionError in <section> ...`; the remaining sections still run, and the run fails at the end if any section failed. Passed checks are counted, failed ones are not.

`python -B -X utf8 tools/migration/goal9b_mutation_check.py` reverts eighteen Goal 9B decisions one at a time in a temporary copy of `src/main/java` under `build/migration/goal9b` and reruns the regression (log: `build/migration/goal9b/mutation_check.log`). The unmutated baseline must pass first. A mutation counts as detected only when the regression reports an `AssertionError`; a mutation that does not compile is reported as an error. `--source DIR` mutates another source root (for example a copy with a proposed fix) and `--log FILE` writes elsewhere.

Expected numbers come from the original at `608fec4`, not from the NEO classes: `TileEntitySteamEngine.java:45-47` (2,000 mB steam, 20 mB spent steam, efficiency 0.85), `:96-119` (ops, power, acceleration, rotor and sound), `:121-136` and `:233-244` (sync and client interpolation), `:166-184` (saved keys), `:139-148` (connections, with `ForgeDirection.getRotation(UP)` written out as NORTH -> EAST -> SOUTH -> WEST -> NORTH); `TileEntityCondenser.java:31-37`, `:62-84`, `:98-122`; `Fluids.java:632-634` (steam: `FT_Coolable(SPENTSTEAM, 100, 1, 200)`, TURBINE 1.0); `AnvilRecipes.java:351-358`.

## Stand-ins

`goal9b_regression/stubs.py` starts from the Goal 9A stand-ins and then:

- registers `MACHINE_STEAM_ENGINE`, `MACHINE_CONDENSER` and the sound `STEAM_ENGINE_OPERATE` as identities; `Level.playSound` records every call (the Goal 8 stand-in);
- adds the vanilla `Mth.clamp(float, float, float)` and `Mth.wrapDegrees(double)` with the bodies of the Minecraft 1.21.1 sources in `build/moddev/artifacts/neoforge-21.1.228-sources.jar` (`net/minecraft/util/Mth.java:136-138` and `:275-283`);
- replaces the buffers with one model: `io.netty.buffer.ByteBuf` keeps one list of typed values with a reader index (int, short, long, float, double, byte, boolean; `RegistryFriendlyByteBuf` adds UTF strings to the same list), `release()` counts calls, `readableBytes()` counts values rather than bytes, and `writeBytes(ByteBuf src)` appends the source's unread values and advances the source's reader index. That last rule is netty's `AbstractByteBuf.writeBytes(ByteBuf)` (`writeBytes(src, src.readableBytes())`, then `src.readerIndex(src.readerIndex() + length)`; lines 830-843 of the netty 4.0.23 sources in the local Gradle cache; the netty version NEO runs on is not available locally as sources, and the contract is the same in its javadoc). `Unpooled.buffer()` returns a new buffer. `values()` is a stand-in accessor that reads the unread values without consuming them. The earlier stand-ins kept two separate lists, which only stayed ordered by accident;
- makes `LoadedBaseBlockEntity` follow production `LoadedBaseBlockEntity.java:82-96`: `getVolume` is 10 % while muffled, `serialize`/`deserialize` carry `muffled` and `tilted`. `networkPackNT` stays a counter and does not serialize: the engine's `serialize` consumes the tick's buffer (`writeBytes`), so section 3 calls it exactly once, directly after the server tick, in place of `networkPackNT`;
- compiles the production `FT_Coolable` on the Goal 9A `FluidTrait`/gson surface and appends the fluids it needs to the `Fluids` stand-in (the earlier IDs stay), with the `FT_Coolable` traits parsed from production `Fluids.java` (every `new FT_Coolable(` must parse, constants such as `eff_steam_turbine` are resolved; a missing or extra definition stops the run).

No older stand-in needed a change for Goal 9B: the Goal 8 chain (and with it Goal 9A) derives the `NtmBlocks`/`NtmItems` names from the live `AnvilRecipes`, so `REINFORCED_STONE`, `MACHINE_STEAM_ENGINE` and `SHELL_STEEL` are declared automatically, and the Goal 3A/5A anvil suites compiled the changed list unchanged.

## Sections

1. Condenser: 100 mB spent steam in, 100 mB water out; `min(spent, water room)` converted 1:1 for six fill cases; `waterTimer` 20 on a conversion, counting 19 .. 0 and back to 20 on a new conversion; marked for saving only on ticks that convert; NBT with the original reversed keys (`water` holds the spent steam tank, `steam` the water tank) and reload; the sync payload (both tanks and `waterTimer`); for each of the six faces a steel barrel feeds spent steam through a duct on that face and a duct on the opposite face takes the water to a second barrel: ten ticks give exactly 1,000 mB in, 900 mB out, with the total kept every tick.
2. Steam engine formula: tank sizes; the parsed steam trait; eight fill cases of `ops = min(steam / 100, spent room)` with 170 HE per op; 0 .. 20 ops each exact (no rounding loss); a full spent tank stops the engine; `powerBuffer` reset every tick and not accumulated; tank types forced back to steam and spent steam.
3. Rotor, sound, save and sync: 1,100 ticks (600 working, 500 idle) against a float model of the original: acceleration +-0.1 clamped to [0, 40], the rotor, a sound exactly on each full turn at the block corner with `BLOCKS`, volume `getVolume(1.0)` (0.1 when muffled) and pitch `0.5 + acceleration / 80` (1.0 at full speed); a stopped engine is silent; marked for saving on working ticks only; NBT `powerBuffer`, `acceleration`, `s`, `w` and no rotor (reload starts the rotor at 0); the previous tick's buffer released; one sync per tick at range 150; the payload order muffled, tilted, steam tank from before the tick's use (2,000 mB while the engine already holds 0), `powerBuffer`, rotor, spent tank; client interpolation over three ticks the short way round (350 -> through 360) and 0 -> 30, 60, 90, then set to the synced angle; the client tick only animates.
4. Connections for all four facings: the three positions and their direction on the clockwise side; for each of the three ways to put steam, spent steam and power on the three positions, a steam barrel and duct feed the engine, a duct takes the spent steam to a barrel and red cables carry 170 HE per 100 mB (3,400 HE/t at full speed) into a battery socket with a lithium pack; the same parts on the mirrored (counter-clockwise) side connect to nothing; power `canConnect` is false up, down and for null; fluids keep the default rule.
5. Anvil: the live list is the Goal 8 list (equal in content and index) plus the boiler and then the steam engine; the engine's inputs, output, tier 2 and construction overlay; a META 1 large gear is rejected, plain and META 0 gears are accepted; one item short of any count is rejected; no entry takes the engine.
6. Chain: coal firebox -> boiler (north) -> steam duct -> engine -> spent steam duct -> condenser -> water duct -> boiler, red cable -> battery socket, 2,000 mB of water, 1,200 ticks. Every tick `100 x (water + spent steam) + steam` summed over the six tanks is unchanged; this is taken after the tick's network transfer, and ducts are connectors without a tank (not `IFluidUserMK2`), so nothing is held in the networks between ticks. Every op's 170 HE reaches the socket; the last 200 ticks are steady at one op and 170 HE/t. All five machines saved after tick 600 and reloaded (ducts and cables rebuilt) continue identically to tick 1,200 (the rotor, which the original does not save, is not compared).
7. Failure chain and capacity: an independent model in the test (firebox +200 TU/t, checked against the live firebox; boiler pull, decay, ops and explosion rule; engine ops; condenser conversion; then the networks: barrel water into the boiler, boiler steam into the engine, spent steam into the condenser) predicts the explosion tick for the condenser removed (engine stops after 20 ops; 16,050) and for the condenser's water outlet blocked (220 ops: its 100 mB of water, then 100 mB of spent steam, then the engine's 20; 16,250). A water barrel keeps the boiler supplied, because the boiler's own 16,000 mB cannot fill 1,600,000 mB of steam once the engine has taken 4,000 mB. The live chain must stop the engine after the same number of ops with a full spent tank and explode on the predicted tick with exactly 1,600,000 mB of steam. One condenser keeps five engines fed from a large steam barrel at 20 ops and 3,400 HE/t each; with six, from tick 3 at most 100 ops a tick in total (below 120), the condenser stays at 100 mB/t, and every op's spent steam is accounted for. Per-engine shares under congestion are not asserted: the network's rounding remainder goes to a randomly chosen provider (`NodeNet.rand`).
